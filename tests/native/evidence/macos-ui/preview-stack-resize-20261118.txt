three cards through CapturePreviewWindow::open. The baseline ran two
light and two dark sessions at the default bottom-right corner. An
opaque center-column run measured 280 pixels in the initial card.
Eight of 12 growth transitions showed a stretched older frame.

The revised build used the same grow commands and app-local Close
clicks to shrink from four cards back to one. Both appearances ran
top-right and bottom-right, with three growth and three shrink
transitions each. The existing card's opaque run stayed within its
normal 280 pixels or became shorter while a card was clipped or
transitioning. All four runs settled to 280 pixels per card at each
count. WindowServer reported the same chosen corner anchor at every
settled size. Retained frames during expansion aligned with the corner
as well. The bottom-aligned early two-card sample had opaque pixels at
rows 312–591 of 600, and the top-aligned sample at rows 8–287 of 600.
