macOS GPUI and Electron blurred image wallpaper comparison, 2026-11-17

Electron 43.4.0 renders an expanded wallpaper image before applying canvas
blur. GPUI previously covered only the final canvas and blurred that crop.
A generated 64x48 high-contrast checker wallpaper, scaled behind a 17,024x128
opaque screenshot with 25px output padding and blur radius 25px, exposed the
difference. In the 25px top margin (426,850 pixels), the maximum per-pixel
channel error against Electron was 43, the mean maximum-channel error was
14.544, and 415,994 pixels differed by more than two levels. The bottom margin
had maximum 32, mean 5.007 and 291,877 pixels over two levels.

GPUI now renders using the expanded cover dimensions and enough surrounding
pixels for the blur kernel, and composites that result once. For the same
input, the top margin maximum fell to 13, mean to 7.495 and count over two to
401,167. The bottom maximum fell to 11, mean to 7.042 and count over two to
397,066. This is an improvement, not a complete match. A 320x180 generated
checker comparison against four independently obtained Electron samples failed
before the change (for example 7 versus 47) and passes within eight levels
with the changed geometry.

Three additional oversized cases used a high-contrast alpha-128 wallpaper at
blur radius 7.5 and 3.4375, and a smooth alpha-128 wallpaper at radius 7.5.
With both screenshot orientations and both exposed 25px margins, the patterned
cases had mean maximum-channel errors of 7.027 to 7.782 and maxima of 12 to 18;
the smooth case had means of 0.814 to 0.843 and a maximum of four. A check with
Electron imageSmoothingQuality set to low rather than high produced identical
output in the opaque patterned case. This does not establish the remaining
cause, which should be investigated rather than attributed to a particular
filter or fixed with a visual tolerance.

The GPUI fixture compared each editor preview with its reopened PNG before and
after panning. Across four runs, both source orientations and opaque/varying
source alpha, all 32 comparisons stayed within two channel levels. Its
translucent background check samples an interior point of the exposed margin;
blurred image edges may legitimately have reduced alpha. This replaces the
previous fixture's assumption that the extreme corner always retains full
wallpaper alpha.

Reproduction uses generated images and isolated profiles. One GPUI case:
PORATAKE_PARITY_OUTPUT=/tmp/poratake-pattern-check PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_FRAMED_ONLY=1 PORATAKE_PARITY_SCALE=1 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_DPI=1.25 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_ZOOM=0.25 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_FRAME=none PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_CORNERS=24 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_IMAGE_ALPHA=255 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_IMAGE_PATTERN=1 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_BACKGROUND_BLUR=40 PORATAKE_PARITY_LARGE_ANNOTATION=ordered-blackout PORATAKE_PARITY_THEME=light src/main/target/debug/poratake-gpui --parity

Use the generated profile's large-wallpaper.png and framed-export-true-false.png:
env -u ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE node_modules/.bin/electron tests/native/electron-wallpaper-blur.cjs <profile>/large-wallpaper.png /tmp/electron-wallpaper.png 17074 178 25
python3 tests/native/compare-wallpaper-margins.py <profile>/framed-export-true-false.png /tmp/electron-wallpaper.png --orientation horizontal --margin 25

The fixtures do not use a live capture, exercise physical displays or establish
complete Electron parity. Peak memory and interactive performance of the
expanded blur surface have not been measured.
