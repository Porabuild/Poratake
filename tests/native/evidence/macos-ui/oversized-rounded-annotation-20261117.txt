macOS GPUI oversized rounded screenshot annotations, 2026-11-17, source d05c98bb

An isolated Metal fixture generated 17,024x128 and 128x17,024 textured opaque
and varying-alpha images. It used a solid wallpaper, padding 20, rounded
corners 24, no shadow and no inset. A blackout, blur or pixelation redaction
spanned the 8,192px tile seam. Removing the redaction changed the exported
pixels for each completed case; the blackout control also confirmed an opaque
black pixel in the saved PNG.

For both app appearances, 25% and 50% zoom, and simulated 100% and 200%
scales, each orientation and alpha case was compared with its reopened PNG
before and after panning. All 192 visible-region comparisons had no pixels
more than two channel levels apart; the maximum difference was two.

At simulated 125%, each of 12 style/appearance/zoom runs stopped on the first
horizontal opaque comparison because of the previously recorded full-preview
mismatch. A second set panned first: all 12 runs compared a 5x5-pixel sample
centered on the redaction at the seam with zero channel difference, but still
failed the full visible-region comparison. At 25% zoom, 14,117 pixels differed
by more than two levels, maximum 79; at 50%, 47,806 differed, maximum 200.
The blackout sample was independently checked to be inside an opaque black area.
Each run stopped at its first mismatch, so these 125% samples do not cover the
other orientation or translucent source. They do not establish full redaction
parity, and the large image mismatch remains open.
