Within that region, unencoded-to-GPUI-decoded mean RGB error was 1.228,
maximum 104. An independent FFmpeg RGB-to-YUV420-to-RGB round trip differed
from the unencoded frame by a mean 1.171, maximum 106; the control and GPUI
decoded frame differed by a mean 0.170, maximum seven. All 469 channels whose
unencoded-to-decoded error exceeded 32 also differed from the independent
conversion by more than 16, and all 469 were within eight levels of the
conversion-only result. These observations are consistent with chroma
subsampling accounting for the large errors in this fixture; they do not prove
that every color difference or every recording has the same cause.

The isolated Electron probe used the exact same full composition and a PNG of
the same FFmpeg 4:2:0 control. It encoded a single 1280x720 frame through
WebCodecs H.264 at 12 Mbps and 30 fps; the local run logged OpenH264. In the
same region its decoded frame differed from the input by mean 1.643, maximum
104, with 333 channels above 32. Only 33 of those channels were within eight
levels of the FFmpeg conversion control; decoded-to-control mean error was
1.389. This is an independent single-frame encoder probe, not the full Electron
editor or exporter, and its encoding settings and timing differ from GPUI's
recording export. The results do not establish exact cross-shell equivalence.
