macOS microphone startup timing, generated fixture, 2026-11-16

No screen or microphone was captured. The native test supplies generated PCM,
timestamps, video frames and clocks to the recorder and decodes its AAC output.
The screen timeline starts at clock time 100 seconds. The microphone is enabled
at timeline time 20 seconds; its first generated sample was captured at 21.
Before the fix the recorder wrote the first sample at 20.01 seconds rather than
the expected 21.01 seconds, and the delayed-start assertion failed.

The corrected test checks the first sample end at 21.01 seconds, the next sample
end at 21.51 seconds, silence at 20.5 seconds and audible PCM at 21.1 and 21.45
seconds. It covers installation of the microphone writer before the first screen
frame and after video has already started without another screen callback.

An additional synthetic timebase maps source timestamp 1021 to screen clock 121
with a source origin of 1000 and screen origin of 100. A delayed callback case
delivers that sample when the screen clock reads 123; it still starts at timeline
time 21. The rate is one: this is an origin-conversion test, not a device drift
or hardware latency test. The generated AAC outputs are retained beside this
file as microphone-startup-shifted-20261116.m4a,
microphone-startup-late-callback-20261116.m4a, and
microphone-startup-initial-20261116.m4a.

The GPUI test static_screen_microphone_toggle_matches_preview_after_trim uses
the shifted-clock fixture in an edited project and checks silence and audible
samples in both preview audio and decoded export. It passed with:

cargo test --offline --manifest-path src/main/app-gpui/Cargo.toml static_screen_microphone_toggle_matches_preview_after_trim -- --ignored --nocapture

Apple documents that sample timestamps from AVCaptureSession outputs use its
synchronization clock:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/avcapturesession/synchronizationclock

Actual microphone startup, capture and screen clock behavior on hardware,
device swaps, clock rate drift, and the unavailable-clock fallback remain open.
