macOS native tray menu to capture selector, 2026-11-18

One built-in Retina display, native GPUI development probe, right-edge
tray geometry, and app-local click on the actual Capture Area row.
An authorized screen-only daemon handled capture. The bridge checked
WindowServer for the menu at receipt of the freeze request. The test
released the selector and any frozen surface after each run.

                          Baseline 5e498e07              Revised
Light live                selector 22.5 ms              selector 33.5 ms
Dark live                 selector 22.4 ms              selector 39.9 ms
Light frozen              selector 188.2 ms             selector 186.6 ms

The old live selector appeared while the menu was visible. The menu
ceased to appear in WindowServer after 115.3 ms in light and 76.9 ms
in dark. In the old frozen runs the tray was visible at the freeze
request and gone by selector visibility. No baseline saved image was
used to infer what the frozen pixels contained. The revised menu was
absent at selector visibility in all four cases, and at the freeze
request in both frozen cases.

The revised driver covered the display with a generated AppKit
backdrop beneath the menu, then selected a 190×160-point area in
the former menu region. Live capture read the display and frozen
capture used the cached frame. After ColorSync conversion to sRGB,
all 121,600 pixels in each of the four resulting 380×320 images were
(30, 86, 147), the backdrop color in this fixture. The probe did not
touch the real clipboard or use physical mouse or keyboard input.

All 1,173 runnable GPUI tests and 70 Electron menu and tray reference
tests passed. Timing samples are individual WindowServer observations
and include app-local dispatch and polling.
