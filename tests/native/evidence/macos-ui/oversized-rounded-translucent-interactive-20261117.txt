An isolated Metal fixture generated textured 17,024x128 and 128x17,024 images
with opaque or varying-alpha pixels. It used an unframed 50%-alpha solid-color
wallpaper, padding 20, rounded corners 24, no inset and no shadow. It asserted
that the exported PNG retained alpha 128 at the outer corner and compared the
visible preview with a reopened full PNG before and after panning to a seam.

A 48-run matrix covered both app appearances, simulated 100%, 125% and 200%
scales, 25% and 50% zoom, and no annotation or a blackout, blur or pixelation
across the tile seam. All 384 orientation/alpha/pan comparisons had no pixels
more than two channel levels apart; the maximum difference was two. Controls
confirmed that the enabled corner and redaction effects changed exported pixels.

A separate 24-run matrix used a solid or 50%-alpha wallpaper in both
appearances, at the same three simulated scales and two zooms. For each of 96
orientation/alpha cases, the fixture sent a GPUI mouse-down and mouse-move to
draw a rectangle, checked that the in-progress draft changed visible pixels,
then sent mouse-up and checked that exactly one rectangle was committed. It
compared the committed preview against the reopened PNG before and after
panning. All 192 visible-region comparisons had no pixels more than two levels
apart; the maximum difference was two. An undo control confirmed that the
rectangle changed the PNG. The fixture does not compare the in-progress draft
against a saved image, because the draft is not committed yet.
