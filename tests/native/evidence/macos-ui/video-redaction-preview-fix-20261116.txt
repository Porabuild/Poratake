macOS GPUI video redaction preview/export, 2026-11-16

Scaled video compositions now replay active blur and pixelation against
source-resolution crops. Intersecting source regions are grouped, and each
affected output rectangle is replaced through a bounded temporary patch. No
device or display access is used. A source-blend operation with a clip mask
changed pixels outside the mask in the zoom/camera fixture, so that approach was
not used; the bounded-patch check requires pixels outside the rectangles to be
unchanged, including with translucent content.

In the generated one-pixel-stripe video from
video-pixelation-scaled-20261116.txt, the original-area-weighted version gave
mean BGR differences of 5.67 for the half-size preview versus the decoded
export and 4.51 for the half-size versus reduced full-size preview at time 0.
With the new path those measurements were 1.838 and 0.000. At 0.25 seconds they
were 1.838 and 0.000; at 0.5 and 1.25 seconds they were 1.878 and 0.018.
At those four times the blur region's preview/full-preview errors were 0.022,
0.022, 0.091 and 0.087; clipped blur's were 0.091, 0.091, 0.081 and 0.083.
The H.264 encoding is lossy. This is generated media, not a live recording or
a physical display comparison. The pinned Electron pixel-sampling difference
noted in the earlier diagnostic remains open.

The generated composition tests cover four clipped edges, negative extents,
roughly 13%, 33%, 50%, 73% and 150% outputs, overlapping pixelation and blur,
annotations before and after effects, zoom, a camera layer, a translucent
background, and separate effects. The two batch tests check separated and
transitively overlapping source regions. The 86 composition tests, 10
pixelation tests, generated edited-project save/reopen and decoded export test,
