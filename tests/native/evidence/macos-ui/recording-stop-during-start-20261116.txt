macOS recorder coordination fixture, 2026-11-16

The permission-free Swift fixture holds a synthetic startup behind a continuation
and issues two Stop requests. Neither backend stop runs before startup finishes;
afterward both callers receive the same result and the backend stops once. It
also checks a failed startup and rejects a stale stop without affecting a newer
session. The fixture completed successfully; the optional ScreenCaptureKit
exclusion check was skipped.

The full Swift daemon was typechecked without launching a capture. This evidence
does not exercise a real capture stream or the GPUI startup-timeout handoff.
