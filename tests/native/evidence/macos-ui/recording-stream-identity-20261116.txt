macOS ScreenCaptureKit recorder: generated-sample and empty-writer checks

The native recording-timeline fixture injects a screen sample associated with
an old stream after activating a different stream. Before the identity guard,
the fixture failed: "A previous stream must not anchor a new recording".
With the guard, the old sample is ignored and the new stream's sample anchors
the timeline at 20 seconds. The remaining generated timeline tests pass.

The recording-empty-video fixture starts a 64x64 AVAssetWriter without
appending a frame, then calls the recorder's stop path. It reports:
  Asset writer error: The operation could not be completed
The recorder returns to idle without reporting successful finalization.

Both checks use generated samples and a local writer; they do not start an
SCStream, access a display, request permissions, or exercise real callbacks.
The Swift daemon typechecks for arm64 and x86_64 macOS. The daemon-platform
unit test passes 11 tests.
