macOS recording outline lifecycle, 2026-11-16

An isolated AppKit fixture created its own generated target window and drove the
real RecordingOverlayModule and its scheduled tracking timer. It did not start a
recording, capture the screen, access media devices or touch an existing editor.
The generated window was moved, ordered out, restored at a new position and closed.

Before the change, after the hidden target left the on-screen window list, the
fixture failed: "temporarily hidden target lost its outline tracker".

After the change, with an explicit check that the outline remained registered for
capture exclusion while hidden and restored:
visible target: outline matches bounds
moved target: outline follows
hidden target: outline hidden, tracker retained
restored target: outline follows new bounds
closed target and explicit cleanup: outline hidden and unregistered

The fixture calls the same outline presentation and cleanup methods, but the
capture-exclusion registry has no live ScreenCaptureKit filter in this test. It
does not establish captured pixels, physical pointer behavior, behavior on
another display, or that a live recording stops and exports after target closure.
