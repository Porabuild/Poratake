by the writer, the generated audio also starts at 20 seconds. The fixture sets
that internal state; it does not exercise actual encoder backpressure.

The generated AAC track was placed in a GPUI recording project and trimmed to
source 19.5–20.75 seconds. The test passed: preview and decoded export were
silent before the toggle and audible after it, with sampled mean levels within
1500 signed 16-bit units.

Real microphone capture, differences between hardware clocks, capture startup
