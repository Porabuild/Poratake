2026-11-18 macOS native capture responsiveness

Apple M5 Pro, macOS 27.2, one built-in Retina display. Current GPUI parity
development app, temporary profiles and generated backdrop, with the previously
authorized screen-only Swift daemon. Six cycles per profile and build. Warm
statistics use the five cycles after the first. Times are milliseconds.

The driver sent app-local commands and polled WindowServer window metadata at
roughly 4 ms intervals. Switching required the old selector to disappear and
the replacement to appear. Thumbnail readiness also required the selector
to disappear. The editor endpoint was the appearance of its native window.
The measurements include dispatch and polling overhead. No physical pointer
input, compositor-paint timestamps or camera capture were involved.

Before selector fix, warm cancel-to-hidden was 166.9 ms for small live,
167.5 ms for large live, and 163.8 ms for large frozen. In the direct light
comparison it fell to 14.7, 12.3, and 13.6 ms. Switching medians fell from
182.7, 177.3, and 167.4 ms to 25.7, 34.9, and 79.2 ms.

Before the editor fix, with the faster selector already applied, dark
preview-to-editor warm medians were 62.6 ms live and 48.0 ms frozen at
3000×1800. The corresponding first opens were 92.9 and 81.4 ms.

One earlier frozen trial took 884.1 ms to a ready thumbnail, with a 509.2 ms
screenshot RPC observed in that timing group. The repeated frozen groups
did not reproduce that tail. These samples give observed ranges and do not
establish a latency bound.
