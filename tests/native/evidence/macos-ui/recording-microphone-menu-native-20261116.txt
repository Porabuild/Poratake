checkerboard preview, an isolated configuration and the fixture daemon described
in scripts/parity/README.md. The daemon returned generated microphone names;
no screen capture, microphone, camera or system-audio capture was started by

inspect them. The helper captured only the generated recording-control window.

to the toolbar width. The AppKit hit test reached the control at its center and
another window four points outside, with zero centering error.

normal-level probe does not establish the production floating window's appearance
