macOS rounded-blackout rasterizer investigation, 2026-11-16

The inputs are synthetic opaque magenta and black rounded rectangles. No screen
capture, media devices, existing editor or privacy settings were used.

The Core Graphics probe draws an opaque black rounded rectangle over opaque
magenta at the same scale, offset and rectangle coordinates as the earlier
blackout-order Electron shape-under-blackout comparison. Across nine cases and
144 red-channel samples, 33 differ from Electron's SVG-image output by over
two levels, with maximum 24 and mean 3.32. The earlier GPUI output had 31 such
samples, maximum 34 and mean 5.08. The top-left pixel at 1x and integer origin
was 182 from Core Graphics, 191 from GPUI and 158 from Electron SVG-image.
Core Graphics is therefore not an exact substitute.

The new Electron probe compares an SVG rounded rectangle, an equivalent SVG
cubic path, a Canvas 2D roundRect and a Canvas cubic path. At 1x and integer
origin, the SVG rectangle top-left 2x2 red patch is [[158, 7], [7, 0]], while
the other three give [[191, 64], [64, 0]]. Across nine cases and 144 samples,
each path form differs from the SVG rectangle in 32 samples by over two levels,
maximum 89. Turning image smoothing off for the SVG image changed none of those
144 samples. The window reported devicePixelRatio 1.
Repeating the Electron run produced identical JSON.

python3 tests/native/evidence/macos-ui/blackout-analytic-comparison.py

The generated candidate analytic ellipse-coverage calculation matched the
integer cases but still differed by up to 12 levels in fractional cases. Skia
documents a related implicit-ellipse/gradient coverage calculation at
https://skia.googlesource.com/skia/+/568c98606fee6ad1b8a0714dd4dffbd4aa1d11d0/src/gpu/instanced/InstanceProcessor.cpp;
that source does not establish which rendering path Electron selected here.
These observations isolate the difference
to the rounded-rectangle rendering path, but do not identify its precise
coverage rule or prove a full editor workflow. No production rendering change
was made.

An expanded Electron SVG matrix contains seven radii from 0.5 through 8, eight
horizontal offsets, four vertical offsets and 12x12 red-channel patches. The
inputs and samples are blackout-coverage-matrix.html and
blackout-coverage-matrix-20261116.json. blackout-coverage-matrix.py compares
the samples with three candidate formulas; blackout-coverage-models-20261116.json
records the results. A second Electron run produced identical matrix JSON.
blackout-tiny-matrix.rs generated the Tiny Skia samples, and
blackout-tiny-matrix-compare.py compares them against Electron. For radius 2,
the implicit-gradient formula differed by
over two levels in 42 of 4,608 samples (maximum 16), a direct signed-distance
coverage differed in 33 (maximum 15), and a piecewise combination differed in
10 (maximum 5). A Tiny Skia scratch renderer using the production rounded-rect
path differed in 229 of the same 4,608 samples (maximum 34). The piecewise
combination is a fitted experiment without a verified source rule and was not
adopted. These are upper-left samples on an opaque backdrop; clipping, other
corners, transparency and preview/export behavior are not established.

The retained outputs are blackout-accelerated-20261116.json and
blackout-software-20261116.json. Electron reported enabled GPU compositing,
2D canvas, rasterization and Skia Graphite in the default run; all four were
disabled in the software run. Repeated samples were identical for each mode.
For radius 2, 117 of 4,608 red-channel samples differed between the modes by
more than two levels, maximum 34. The 1x integer-origin top-left pixel was 158
in the accelerated run and 169 in software. Software reproduces the earlier
unattributed 169 value, but does not establish where that assertion came from.

The radius-2 Tiny Skia matrix differed from the accelerated run by more than two
levels in 229 samples, maximum 34, and from software in 242 samples, maximum 56.
These tests use a synthetic SVG image and opaque magenta backdrop, not either
full editor, a physical display comparison, other corners, transparency or a
recorded screenshot. No production rasterizer change was made.
