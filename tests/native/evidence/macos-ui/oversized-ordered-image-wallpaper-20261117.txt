macOS GPUI oversized rounded screenshot and translucent blur, 2026-11-17

An isolated Metal fixture used generated 17,024x128 and 128x17,024 screenshots
with either opaque or varying-alpha source pixels. It used an unframed image,
20px padding, 24px rounded corners, no shadow or inset, and a generated 64x48
image wallpaper with alpha 128 or 255. The red stripe, redaction, and green
rectangle overlap at the 8,192px tile seam, in that order. Export assertions
check the final green rectangle, a blacked-out part of the preceding stripe, and
a control export with the redaction removed. All 60 controls changed 2,168
pixels in the redaction bounds.

Twelve runs covered blackout, blur and pixelation; simulated scales 1.25 and 2;
and wallpaper alpha 128 and 255. The alpha-128 runs used light appearance and
25% zoom; the alpha-255 runs used dark appearance and 50% zoom. Three further
runs covered the same redactions with a blurred alpha-128 image wallpaper at
simulated 1.25 scale (light/25% for blackout and pixelation; dark/50% for blur).
For both source orientations and alpha patterns, the fixture compared visible
preview pixels with the reopened PNG before and after panning to the seam. All
120 comparisons had zero pixels more than two levels apart; the maximum channel
difference was two. Outer wallpaper alpha was checked in every saved PNG.

Before the fix, the blurred alpha-128 wallpaper exported alpha 192: GPUI drew
the blurred pixels over the original. A gradient regression also failed before
the fix. At e9d24294 the shared background painter blurred in place, and that
regression passed for alpha 0, 64, 128 and 255 while checking that blur changes
pixels. A later wallpaper-blur-cover-20261117.txt comparison changed the
geometry to include the expanded source used by Electron.
Electron's wallpaper renderer applies blur to a single background draw; these
fixtures do not establish exact visual equivalence to Electron's blur kernel or
image cover calculation. The blur fix is e9d24294.

An existing composed, unrounded oversized seam fixture was rerun after sharing
the ordered annotation setup. It passed at simulated 1.25 scale and 50% zoom
with a blackout, both source orientations and both source alpha patterns.
