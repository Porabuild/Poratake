macOS GPUI preview audio replay, generated stereo PCM

The video editor keeps decoded audio stems in rodio players. The software-mixer
test played a generated 0.05-second stem to completion and then sought to its
start. Before the fix, rodio had removed the source and the seek returned
without queuing audio; the test failed because the player remained empty.
After the fix, the source was queued again and the mixer produced audio.

A second software-mixer test drained program and music stems, scrubbed to
0.025 seconds, and heard the second half of the program stem while music
remained silent. It then exhausted that stem and resumed playback from the same
offset, with both stems queued and their samples present in the mix. The ten
preview-audio tests passed on the Mac.

An existing generated audio workflow separately builds preview stems, applies
a cut and volume changes, saves/reopens the project, exports H.264/AAC and
checks decoded audio against the preview mix. It does not test physical playback
or the audio device. No microphone, camera or system-audio capture was used.

The parity development executable also completed an isolated fake-daemon
screen, area and window recording/editor handoff with a generated silent video.
It compared edited previews with decoded exports; it did not exercise replay of
recorded audio or live capture.
