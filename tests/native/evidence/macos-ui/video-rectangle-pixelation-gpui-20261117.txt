macOS isolated GPUI recording/editor verification, 2026-11-17

The parity fixture used an isolated profile, its fake daemon, and the same
1280x720 generated checker video for area, window and screen handoffs. The
video was upscaled from the repository's 320x180 capture.mov fixture with
FFmpeg's nearest-neighbor filter and encoded with h264_videotoolbox. Its SHA-256
in this run was 16f6b9f228a62082ffa240669aa5c065fa2c5aac37c2f5badbcee30193236ec4.
The encoder output is not expected to be byte-identical on other machines.

For each target the fixture simulates drawing a filled rectangle and then
pixelation across its bottom-right corner, verifies both saved annotations,
reopens the project, and initiates a scrub. The resulting 640x360 composition
must exactly match the editor's preview image bytes and the pixelation must
change at least 20 pixels. At 1 second, the captured Metal-rendered preview is
compared to the scaled composition, an unredacted control and the decoded
1280x720 H.264 export. The affected rectangle is measured separately.

Three target fixtures produced identical measurements because they used the
same source and edits:

The area, window and screen fixture paths passed in both runs. The original
recording-only fixture also passed with its default source and without the new
redaction option. Some expected error messages are emitted while the fixture
checks recovery from fake recording failures.

The dark and light screenshots are video-rectangle-pixelation-gpui-dark-20261117.png
and video-rectangle-pixelation-gpui-light-20261117.png. The representative
video-rectangle-pixelation-decoded-20261117.png is an FFmpeg-decoded frame at
one second; numerical comparisons use the app's VideoDecoder.

The setup and commands are in scripts/parity/README.md. The fixture uses a fake
daemon and generated media; it does not verify physical capture, actual target
movement or closure, microphone, camera, system audio, overlay exclusion, or a
physical multi-display transition. The screenshots are GPUI test-renderer
outputs, not captures of the macOS desktop.
