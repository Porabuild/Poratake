macOS native recording menu resize and paint samples, 2026-11-18

Baseline 498b0731 and a build with top-aligned recording toolbar layer
contents. The probe used the built-in Retina display, the floating
AppKit toolbar, app-local input, and an isolated daemon fixture that
supplied microphone and camera lists. The iOS list was empty. A separate
screen-only daemon with prior authorization captured only the probe's
toolbar window by ID. No media devices were accessed.

Each theme and build ran 21 menu openings, split into sessions of
three and four cycles through microphone, camera, and iOS. The driver
waited for expanded WindowServer geometry, then sampled the PNG's
alpha below the toolbar to detect a menu. A resized surface containing
the scaled compact toolbar covered at least 85 percent of that sample's
canvas, and was counted separately. Representative frames were also
inspected visually.

Baseline, light                     21    2 / 79
Baseline, dark                      21    0 / 69
Revised, light                      21    0 / 77
Revised, dark                       21    0 / 80

One earlier exploratory dark sample also captured a stretched frame.
The paired baseline did not reproduce it in every session. Both builds
kept the toolbar centered and produced the expected visible menus. Long
microphone and camera names were ellipsized. The iOS menu showed None.
Dismissal restored the compact 276×44-point toolbar frame.

The times include WindowServer polling, screenshot RPC and the sampling
interval. They are observations of a captured frame with menu pixels.
They do not establish the first scan-out timestamp or a speedup. The
samples use one display and generated pointer events.

Both final offscreen Metal comparisons passed in serial. All 18 light
and 18 dark toolbar snapshot PNGs matched the baseline byte for byte.
A concurrent baseline run had observed Stopping at the fixture's
immediate Idle assertion. The serial rerun completed. All 1,173 runnable
GPUI tests and 76 Electron recording-control reference tests passed.
