macOS recording area bounds, 2026-11-16

The macOS recorder translated an area selection into display-local coordinates
without checking whether the selection still fit the selected display. A stale
selection could therefore produce a negative source origin or extend past the
display after its geometry changed. The recorder now rejects empty, non-finite
or out-of-display areas with CONFIGURATION_ERROR before creating its output
writer. Window capture continues to use its window filter rather than this area
check.

The native fixture covers a valid area and whole display at a negative desktop
origin; areas extending past each edge; zero width; and a non-finite coordinate.
The out-of-display assertion failed with the earlier unchecked calculation. It
also compares the connected main display's AppKit-to-desktop conversion with
Core Graphics metadata and accepts that full display. One display was connected.
No Screen Recording permission or pixel capture was requested.
