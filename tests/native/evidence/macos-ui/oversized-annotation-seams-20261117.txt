macOS GPUI oversized screenshot annotation seams, 2026-11-17, source 8d940eb6

An isolated Metal fixture used generated 17,024x128 and 128x17,024 images,
with opaque or varying-alpha textured pixels over a solid wallpaper. A red
stripe, a redaction and a green rectangle were applied in that order, crossing
the first tile seam. The comparison crop was 512x128 or 128x512. No frames or
wallpaper padding were used.

For each of blackout, pixelation and blur, the fixture checked both image
orientations, both alpha variants, 25% and 50% zoom, simulated display scales
1x, 1.25x and 2x, and both light and dark appearance: 144 cases total.

- The large preview matched the smaller reference preview within one channel
  level in the sampled interior, including the seam and annotation region.
- Every pixel of the corresponding exported PNG crop matched the smaller
  reference export exactly.
- The sampled large preview and smaller reference preview each matched their
  own reopened PNG within one channel level after panning the large image.
- Comparing each export to an otherwise identical unredacted control showed
  2,167 or 2,168 changed pixels. Blackout covered a sampled pixel of the red
  stripe with opaque black; a sampled pixel in the later green shape remained
  green. Blur and pixelation also retained a nonblack sampled filtered pixel.
