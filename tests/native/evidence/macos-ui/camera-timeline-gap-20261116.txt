Missing camera-track warning, 2026-11-16

The Swift stop result now includes cameraTrackMissing when a camera recorder was
started but no usable camera movie was finalized. Both the stop response and the
stopped event carry the flag. Electron retains it through stop and displays a
warning after saving; GPUI parses it in immediate and asynchronous stop responses
and shows a warning after saving. Both skip the warning on deliberate discard.

The native canceled-camera stop fixture asserts the flag while decoding the
primary frame. The GPUI recording_stop_info_preserves_camera_track_warning Rust

recording-actions test files passed 117 tests, including result propagation,

and do not show the GPUI warning or exercise a live camera failure.
