macOS generated video redaction sampling investigation, 2026-11-17

The 4,096-case diagnostic described in video-redaction-rectangle-corners-fix-
20261117.txt found 17 channels more than one level away from the resized full
composition, maximum two. They occurred in seven generated cases: 66, 123, 333,
876, 1110, 2763 and 3792. This is a synthetic composition comparison, not a
live capture or decoded-export check.

The seven cases were rerun with individual changes to the generated scenes:
baseline: 17 channels; camera disabled: 11; camera shadow disabled: 11; camera
corners disabled: 15; zoom disabled: zero; rectangles removed: 16. Each variant
was compared with its own full composition. These interventions do not prove
the cause of every mismatch or establish that all zoomed scenes differ.

For cases 66 and 333, the full-resolution source and the corresponding cropped
composition were compared before output scaling. With drawings and camera
removed, respectively 63 and 84 channels differed, each by at most one level.
With all layers present, case 333 reached a two-level difference. Disabling
zoom eliminated differences in the two sampled source regions. Removing both
video and camera also produced identical sampled source regions.

Combining the zoom transforms in a trial did not remove the output mismatches
in cases 66 and 333 and increased one sampled source region's differences.
Skipping rectangular clipping in another trial did not change those results.
Both trials were discarded. The mechanism and a performant fix remain open.

Reproduce the two source comparisons in an isolated clean checkout:

git apply tests/native/evidence/macos-ui/video-redaction-zoom-edge-diagnostic-20261117.patch
cargo test --offline --locked --manifest-path src/main/Cargo.toml -p poratake-gpui generated_redaction_matrix_matches_full_composition_at_effect_boundaries -- --nocapture

The diagnostic patch changes the test to report mismatches rather than assert
on them, restricts rendering to cases 66 and 333, and renders a full frame for
every redaction batch. It is slow diagnostic instrumentation, not a production
change. To rerun the seven-case controls, replace the selected case list with
the seven IDs above and set PORATAKE_RED_DIAG to camera-off, shadow-off,
corners-off, zoom-off or rectangles-off. The patch must be discarded afterward.
