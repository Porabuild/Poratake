macOS GPUI Metal generated recording handoff and rendered-preview comparison — 2026-11-17

Three distinct synthetic 320x180, 30 fps, two-second sources were generated with
FFmpeg 9.0.2 lavfi testsrc (screen), testsrc2 (area), and smptehdbars (window),
encoded with h264_videotoolbox. A fixture daemon copied each named source into
its corresponding recording project; no screen, microphone, camera, or system audio was captured.

Each project received a 1.5x zoom, a red rectangle, and two video-track cuts;
the 0.75–1.00s source segment was removed. State was saved and reopened before
export. Each decoded export is 320x180, 53 frames at 30 fps (1.766667s).
The fixture verifies each project source frame against its assigned input and
requires the three decoded exports to differ. It compares in-memory previews
with decoded frames at 0.5, 1.0, and 1.5s. At 1.0s it additionally crops the
actual GPUI Metal editor screenshot using the measured preview bounds, scales
it to the decoded frame with a triangle filter, and compares RGB channels.
Channels above 12 are reported alongside the mean; the limit checks mean < 5.

The following fixture recorder failure messages are deliberate fault injections.
Physical capture, controls/overlay exclusion, live target movement and closure,
device audio, actual playback, and physical displays are not established.
