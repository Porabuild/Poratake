macOS screenshot wallpaper image sampling, 2026-11-17

A generated 64x48 wallpaper with varying color and alpha was scaled through
Electron 43.4.0's actual renderBackgroundImageToCanvas function to a 320x180
canvas, with 40px expansion and its blur filter disabled to isolate sampling.
On four independently recorded interior pixels, the GPUI image-wallpaper painter
failed an at-most-four-level RGBA comparison before this change (for example,
[230, 36, 200, 135] versus Electron's [240, 34, 202, 135]). It passes after
using tiny-skia's bicubic sampling, which corresponds to Electron's requested
high-quality scaling. The video editor retains its previous image sampler.

As a broader isolated sampler probe using tiny-skia's PNG decoder rather than
the GPUI image loader, an opaque checker at the same size had no pixels more
than two levels different from Electron in either 320x90 half. A varying-alpha
image had 18 and 20 pixels over two levels in the respective 28,800-pixel
halves. Straight-alpha RGB in nearly transparent pixels can differ much more;
the maximum was 255. This is not a claim of exact equivalence for all images.

The real GPUI editor was also exercised in isolated light and dark fixtures.
For generated patterned translucent wallpapers with and without blur, its full
preview matched the reopened PNG within two levels at simulated 125% and 200%
scales in all eight cases. The fixture now composites partially transparent
blurred gradient edges over the editor's actual matte before comparing; the old
check incorrectly assumed the entire gradient export was opaque.

The generated 690x410 export's 25px margins were compared against Electron
without blur. The GPUI image's mean maximum-channel differences were 2.921 and
3.041, with 5,394 and 6,492 of 17,250 pixels over two levels. A standalone
bilinear tiny-skia rendering of the same source had means 8.022 and 7.761, and
12,553 and 12,303 pixels over two. The standalone probe uses tiny-skia's PNG
decoder, while GPUI uses its own decoder, so the two measurements are not an
isolated sampler-only benchmark. With blur the real GPUI means were 3.088 and
3.052, maxima 10 and 11. At 720x440, the unblurred means were 2.881 and 3.028;
blurred means were 3.018 and 3.090. RGBA comparisons use straight-alpha PNG
values, including colors on almost transparent pixels.

The prior 17,074x178 patterned wallpaper mismatch is already present before
blur. With blur disabled but the same expanded cover geometry, the standalone
bilinear sampler differed from Electron by means 8.066 and 7.076 and maxima
26 and 24 in the 25px top and bottom margins. At x=250 and x=1200, all 178
sampled Electron grayscale values ended in 15 modulo 16. A separately evaluated
model that floors each interpolation fraction to 1/16 matched 66,127 sampled
Electron grayscale values within one level. This is evidence of quantized
sampling in that case, not a verified cross-device rule. At 8192x180 Electron's
high and low smoothing outputs differ; at 17074x178 they were identical on
this Mac, whose WebGL maximum texture size reported 16384. Disabling hardware
acceleration did not change the 17074x178 unblurred reference. A fixed cutoff or
quantized replacement was not added on the basis of this one environment.

With bicubic screenshot sampling, four oversized GPUI fixture runs still passed
all 32 preview/reopened-PNG comparisons within two levels. Their patterned
Electron margin differences ranged from 4.496 to 10.306 mean maximum-channel
levels and up to 24 in one pixel; this remains open. Their smooth alpha-image
case stayed within four levels. The new sampling changes this oversized result
in both directions, so the earlier numbers in wallpaper-blur-cover-20261117.txt
are historical measurements of the preceding implementation.

Native sampler reproduction after an offline parity build (select the built
tiny-skia rlib in src/main/target/debug/deps):
rustc -O --edition 2021 tests/native/wallpaper-sampling.rs -L dependency=src/main/target/debug/deps --extern tiny_skia=<path-to-libtiny_skia.rlib> -o /tmp/wallpaper-sampling
/tmp/wallpaper-sampling <generated-wallpaper.png> /tmp/gpui-sampler.png bilinear 17074 178 50
env -u ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE node_modules/.bin/electron tests/native/electron-wallpaper-blur.cjs <generated-wallpaper.png> /tmp/electron-sampler.png 17074 178 25 --unfiltered
python3 tests/native/compare-wallpaper-margins.py /tmp/gpui-sampler.png /tmp/electron-sampler.png --orientation horizontal --margin 25

These generated offscreen checks do not verify physical displays, live capture,
all wallpaper sources or sustained interactive performance. Standalone 4K
bicubic rendering plus PNG writing took 0.42 seconds in one local run; this is
not an editor responsiveness or peak-memory measurement.
