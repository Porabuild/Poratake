macOS GPUI oversized unframed screenshot preview, 2026-11-17, source b1482c34

An isolated Metal fixture generated textured 17,024x128 and 128x17,024 images
with opaque or varying-alpha pixels. It used an unframed solid wallpaper with
padding 20, rounded corners 24, no inset and no shadow. Before the change, the
oversized backdrop was submitted as one texture and was missing in the preview.
At simulated 100% and 25% zoom, the first horizontal opaque comparison with
the reopened PNG had 8,800 pixels more than two channel levels apart, maximum
87. The editor now tiles the separate backdrop on macOS and retires those tiles
when the backdrop changes.

For dark and light appearances, 25% and 50% zoom, and simulated 100% and 200%
scales, the four orientation/alpha cases were compared before and after
panning to a tile seam. All 64 visible-region comparisons had no pixels more
than two channel levels apart; the maximum difference was two. Removing the
corner effect changed the exported pixels in each case. The comparison covers
only the visible portion of the generated image.

At simulated 125%, both appearances still failed on the first horizontal,
opaque case: 17,218 pixels differed by more than two levels at 25% zoom
(maximum 123), and 45,978 at 50% zoom (maximum 171). The fixture stops each
run at the first mismatch, so the remaining orientation, alpha and panning cases
at 125% were not checked. The separately scaled image and wallpaper do not
always produce the same sampled pixels as the flattened PNG. An experimental
shared-grid adjustment reduced the first 50% case to two mismatched boundary
rows but did not eliminate the discrepancy; it was not kept.

A separate 60-run matrix checked both appearances, 25% and 50% zoom and
simulated 100%, 125% and 200%. It covered (corners, shadow, inset) settings
(0,50,0), (0,50,8), (24,0,8), (24,50,0) and (24,50,8). The same four
orientation/alpha cases were compared before and after panning. All 480
visible-region comparisons had no pixels more than two levels apart; the
maximum difference was one. The 144 corner-off and 192 shadow-off controls
each changed exported pixels. This does not cover every effect setting.
