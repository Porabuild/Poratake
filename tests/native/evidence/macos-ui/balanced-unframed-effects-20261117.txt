macOS GPUI balanced unframed screenshot effects, 2026-11-17

Electron's useCanvasExport computes the balance crop before drawing each image
with its inset, corner radius and shadow. GPUI previously considered balance
with rounded corners and zero padding inactive, so an unframed 200x160 source
exported at 200x160 rather than the expected balanced 140x120. A new export
test reproduced that failure before the change. It now passes with and without
an 8px inset and shadow.

most one pixel may differ by three levels, and all others by at most two.

The fixture uses a scratch profile and generated content. It does not verify a
physical DPI transition, live capture, interactive input, all zooms or
