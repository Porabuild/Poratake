macOS generated video composition verification, 2026-11-17

The redaction source crop includes the coverage of intersecting rectangle
corners. Coverage is derived from the renderer's corner radius, stroke width
and one pixel of antialiasing support. Distant corners do not expand the crop.

The original cases 121 and 536 were added to the regular regression matrix.
Before the change, their largest output differences were three and 12 channel
levels, respectively. The regular matrix now passes within one level in its
selected effect areas and exactly outside them.
